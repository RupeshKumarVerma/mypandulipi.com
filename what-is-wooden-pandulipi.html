The MyPandulipi guide

What is a wooden pandulipi?

A wooden pandulipi is a manuscript-style presentation of a granth: printed pages held between wooden covers and joined with thread. It brings a traditional visual form to scriptures you can read, keep and gift today.

Traditional inspiration, a contemporary product

MyPandulipi products are modern printed editions inspired by manuscript presentation. They are not antique manuscripts or handwritten originals. The wooden cover and thread binding give the book its distinctive appearance.

Choose the scripture first

For a familiar daily recitation, explore Hanuman Chalisa or another Chalisa. For a longer path, see Sundarkand. The Bhagavad Gita in Hindi is a choice for reflective reading, while Bhaktamar Stotra is part of our collection for Jain readers.

Check the edition and presentation

Before ordering, compare the inside-page photographs, language, dimensions and packaging. Mini pandulipis, larger editions and silver-finish books are different formats. Ask for the current edition details rather than assuming every product has the same size or contents.

A gift chosen with care

Think about the recipient's reading habits and spiritual tradition. A compact Chalisa, an Aarti Sangrah for family prayers or a premium gift-box granth can each suit a different occasion. For corporate or bulk gifting, share the quantity and event date with us before finalising your selection.

Keep it ready for the next reading

Store the pandulipi in a dry place, handle the pages gently and keep it away from water and open flames. Retain its gift box for protection when it is not in use.
