Talk to our team
How do I place an order?+

Choose a granth and tap “Enquire on WhatsApp”. Share your quantity and delivery city. Our team confirms availability, the current quotation and delivery details before you order.

Can I see the inside pages?+

Yes. Every product page has a photo gallery. Tap a thumbnail to change the view, or tap the main image to see it larger. You can also ask us for photos of the current edition on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver outside Indore?+

Yes, we ship across India. Delivery is free within Indore; charges may apply outside Indore. Share your PIN code with our team for delivery charges and an estimated arrival date.

Can I order gifts in bulk?+

Yes. We help with family gifting, weddings, religious occasions and corporate orders. You can request a mix of granths and discuss suitable packaging with us.

Are these handwritten manuscripts?+

Our wooden pandulipis are modern printed editions inspired by traditional manuscript presentation, with wooden covers and thread-bound pages. They are not antique manuscripts or handwritten originals. Silver-finish editions have a different page finish and binding.
