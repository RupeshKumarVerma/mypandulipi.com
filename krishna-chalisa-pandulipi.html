About this edition+

For every Krishna bhakt. Made for daily path and Janmashtami, in a Shri Krishna gift box. The wooden cover, red-and-gold thread binding and illustrated box give this mini pandulipi a traditional presentation. Use the open-page photograph to check the printed text and layout. For gifting several pieces, tell us your quantity and occasion so we can confirm available packing options.

Gifting & delivery+

Individual purchases and bulk gifting enquiries are welcome. Share your occasion, delivery city and preferred date. We will confirm the packaging, availability and delivery details on WhatsApp.

Care for your pandulipi+

Keep it in a clean, dry place and turn the pages gently. Protect it from water, incense ash and open diya flames. Keep the gift box for storage between readings.
